Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Junglizer collects nothing. The plugin makes no network connections, and nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

This policy covers the Junglizer plugin (AUv2, VST3 and CLAP for macOS) and this website, getjunglizer.com. Junglizer is made by mrballistic (“we,” “us” or “our”).

1. The plugin

Junglizer runs entirely on your Mac, inside your DAW. It contains no networking code, so it never connects to the internet. Specifically, it has:

  • No analytics, usage tracking or telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No update checks
  • No license activation, accounts or sign-in
  • No third-party services or advertising

The breaks ship inside the plugin, so it doesn’t download anything either. Your phrases, markers, placements and locks are saved inside your DAW project by your host, not by us. When you export MIDI, Junglizer writes the file to ~/Music/Junglizer/Exports on your Mac and shows it in Finder. That file stays on your Mac.

2. This website

If you accept analytics cookies, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google processes that data under its own privacy policy. If you decline, Google’s code isn’t loaded at all and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser so we don’t ask again, and you can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Nothing from the plugin is ever sent to the website.

3. Downloads

The plugin zips and their checksums are served directly from this site, not from a third-party host. Downloading doesn’t require an account, an email address or any other personal details.

4. Children’s privacy

Because the plugin collects nothing, it doesn’t knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under the age required where you live). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we’ll delete it.

5. Your rights

Since the plugin doesn’t collect or store any personal data, there’s nothing for you to access, correct or delete. To remove website analytics, decline in “Cookie settings” and clear this site’s cookies in your browser.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
